Financial & Growth Reality

The Marketplace Trap vs. Your Own Online Store

Why relying 100% on marketplaces caps your brand growth, eats your profit margins, and leaves your business vulnerable.

Selling on Amazon / Flipkart

You do all the hard work manufacturing and packaging, but the marketplace pockets the cream of your profit.

  • 15% to 30% Marketplace Commissions: Heavy referral fees, closing fees, and collection cuts eat your profit on every single transaction.
  • Zero Customer Data: You never get customer phone numbers or email addresses. You cannot send WhatsApp offers or build brand loyalty.
  • Competitors on Your Own Product Pages: Marketplaces place paid ads for cheaper competing items directly below your product pictures.
  • Fake Returns & Penalty Charges: Disproportionate return fees and fraudulent customer claims cost you heavy return shipping bills.
  • Delayed Cash Flow: Payouts take 7 to 15 days to reach your account, choking your working capital.

Your Custom Online Store (D2C)

Direct relationship with your customers, 100% profit retention, and instant settlements straight to your current account.

  • 0% Commission on Sales: Keep 100% of your selling price. Pay only standard UPI/gateway interchange (0% - 1.5%).
  • 100% Customer Data Ownership: Build your own verified buyer list with mobile numbers, shipping addresses, and purchase history.
  • Exclusive Brand Showcase: Only your products are shown. No competitor ads, no distraction, and zero price wars.
  • Automated WhatsApp Re-Orders: Trigger repeat order broadcasts, festival offers, and abandoned cart alerts with a 90%+ open rate.
  • T+1 Instant Bank Deposits: Payments from Razorpay or Paytm settle directly into your bank account on the next business day.
