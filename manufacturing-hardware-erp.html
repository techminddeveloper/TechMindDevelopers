Is Your Factory Unit Losing Margins To These 6 Daily Shop-Floor Headaches?

Managing raw metal melting, outside job-work challans, and manual karigar diaries on paper costs lakhs in unnoticed leakages. Here is how our custom ERP fixes them permanently.

Raw Material vs Finished Goods Wastage & Scrap Loss

Foundries and casting units lose track of exact brass, zinc, or iron weights issued versus finished castings returned. Our ERP calculates precise burning loss percentages and tracks reusable scrap so metal leakage stops immediately.

Untracked Outside Job-Work Challan Management

Sending hardware pieces outside for electroplating, powder coating, or polishing often results in delayed returns and missing inventory. The system tracks every outward challan, vendor loss limit, and pending inward receipt in real time.

Worker Piece-Rate Wages & Karigar Disputes

Manual paper khata entries for buffing, grinding, and machining workers lead to daily arguments and inflated wage payouts. Our software logs worker piece counts against job cards with manager approval, keeping payouts 100% transparent.

Factory Production Tracking & Godown Mismatch

Sales teams commit orders assuming stock is ready, only to find assembly incomplete or raw materials missing in the godown. The ERP links purchase orders, assembly lines, and warehouse bins so stock counts match reality.

Complex Export Billing & Container Packing Lists

Preparing export packing lists with box dimensions, gross and net weights, HS codes, and foreign currency invoices takes hours in Excel. The ERP automates commercial invoices, container packing slips, and shipping marks in 1 click.

Exorbitant "Per-User" ERP Subscriptions

Generic enterprise software demands lakhs in upfront licensing and heavy monthly renewal fees per computer. We build tailor-made manufacturing ERP on a one-time development fee with 100% source code ownership and zero monthly rent.
