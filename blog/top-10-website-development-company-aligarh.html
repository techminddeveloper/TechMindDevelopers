So before you make this important decision, here are the 6 things you absolutely must evaluate:

Custom Code vs. Templates

Does the company build custom websites from scratch, or do they just apply a downloaded template? Custom code means better performance, SEO, and uniqueness.

SEO Knowledge

Can they explain how they will help your website rank on Google? If they cannot answer this clearly, your website will likely never be found by new customers.

Mobile Testing

Ask to see their client websites on your smartphone right now. If the sites look broken or slow on mobile, yours likely will too.

Real Client Reviews

Ask for 2-3 client references you can call directly. Any honest company will provide these without hesitation.

Post-Launch Support

What happens if something breaks after your website launches? Is there a direct WhatsApp number or do they disappear after payment?

Clear Written Agreement

Is everything - timelines, deliverables, payment terms, revision rounds - written clearly in a formal agreement? Never work without one.
