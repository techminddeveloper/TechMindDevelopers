For more than a century, Aligarh has been proudly celebrated across India and the globe as the "Tala Nagari" (City of Locks). From iconic heavy brass padlocks and precision mortise door locks to luxury architectural door handles and tower bolts, Aligarh's craftsmanship is unmatched.

Today, from historic lock-crafting workshops in Upper Fort (Upar Kot) and Shahjamal to large-scale modern manufacturing units across Tala Nagari Industrial Area, Delhi Road, Pala Road, and GT Road, Aligarh produces millions of locks, mortise handles, and architectural hardware fittings every single month. However, many successful factory owners still face daily headaches: managing complex electroplating job-work, tracking raw metal melting loss, and chasing pending dealer payments across India.

In this guide, we break down how modern custom software, automated dealer portals, and digital export catalogs can help Aligarh hardware and lock manufacturers eliminate factory bottlenecks and grow their revenue 10x.

3 Big Bottlenecks Aligarh Hardware Factories Face Every Day

1. Raw Metal Weight & Scrap Accounting

Whether you work with brass ingots, zinc pressure die-casting, aluminum, or stainless steel, raw metal prices fluctuate daily. When raw ingots are melted, machined, and buffed, calculating the exact scrap percentage and melting burning loss on paper registers is almost impossible.

2. Multi-Stage Job-Worker Electroplating Confusion

Lock bodies, handles, and escutcheon plates often travel outside the main factory for specialized nickel plating, antique brass finish, PVD coating, or powder coating. Without digital gate passes, batches frequently get delayed, mixed up, or lost at external job-worker units.

3. Dealer Outstanding Balances & Scheme Tracking

Managing hundreds of hardware stockists across Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and tier-2 markets through phone calls and manual ledgers creates cash flow delays. Dealers constantly call your office just to ask for their outstanding balance, discount scheme status, or transport bilty number.

The 4-Step Digital Blueprint for Aligarh Manufacturers

1. Custom Factory ERP - Designed for Metal & Lock Production

Generic accounting software like Tally is built for basic shop trading, not multi-stage hardware manufacturing. A tailor-made factory ERP tracks:

  • Raw Metal Batch Yield: Input vs output weight calculation to prevent metal leakage.
  • Digital Job Cards: Barcode-tagged batches for casting, pressing, assembly, and packaging.
  • Worker Piece-Rate Payroll: Automated daily piece wages for master fitters, buffing operators, and packers.

2. 24/7 Dealer Self-Service & WhatsApp Automation

Imagine your hardware dealers across India receiving automated WhatsApp notifications with their dispatch invoice, lorry receipt (Bilty) number, and transport tracking the minute their carton leaves your Tala Nagari warehouse.

Dealers can simply send a message on WhatsApp to view their current ledger balance and download seasonal trade discount schemes instantly.

3. High-Speed Digital Product Catalog & B2B Export Website

Large infrastructure builders, interior architects, and export buyers in the Gulf, US, and Europe search online before placing bulk hardware orders. A fast, modern catalog website displaying your complete collection of:

  • Mortise Door Handles (Rose & Plate models)
  • Main Door Digital & Mechanical Locks
  • Concealed Hinges, Tower Bolts & Aldrops
  • Custom Brass Architectural Fittings

allows international and institutional buyers to request formal GST quotation sheets directly with 1 click.
