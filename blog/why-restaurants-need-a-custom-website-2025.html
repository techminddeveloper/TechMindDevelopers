Why Every Restaurant and Cafe Needs Its Own Direct Ordering Website

July 26, 2026 5 min read ... views Tech Mind Developers Team

If you own a restaurant, cafe, or bakery, you know how hard it is to maintain high profit margins. While food delivery apps like Zomato and Swiggy bring new customers, they also take 25% to 30% commission on every single order. Over a year, this commission can cost a restaurant lakhs of rupees in lost profit.

Having your own direct ordering website allows your regular customers to order directly from you - saving you thousands in commissions while building a direct relationship with your customers.

3 Big Reasons Every Restaurant Needs Its Own Website

1. Save 25% to 30% on Every Food Order

When a loyal customer orders their favorite dinner from your own website, you pay zero marketplace commissions. You keep 100% of your food revenue, directly into your bank account via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe) or card.

2. Direct Table Booking and Digital QR Menus

A modern website lets dining guests reserve tables online and view your digital menu with high-resolution food photos. Diners in your restaurant can scan a simple QR code on their table to browse and order directly from their phones.

3. Build Your Own Customer Contact List

Third-party food apps never share customer phone numbers or email addresses with you. With your own website, you own your customer database. You can send weekend discounts, birthday offers, and festival specials directly on WhatsApp to bring customers back every week.
