Hardware ERP

How Custom ERP Software Digitizes Hardware and Lock Manufacturing Units

August 26, 2026 6 min read Tech Mind Developers Team

Precision metal manufacturing, architectural hardware, brass components, and security locks-concentrated in industrial centers like Aligarh (Tala Nagari, Delhi Road) and regional industrial estates-form the backbone of India's building material and security sector.

Operating a precision hardware factory requires coordinating raw metal casting, stamping, CNC machining, electroplating, assembly, and dealer packaging. Relying on manual registers creates bottlenecks that cost factories thousands in delayed orders.

Key Challenges in Hardware and Metal Component Production

  • Raw Material Metal Weight Variance: Brass, zinc, and stainless steel ingots fluctuate in daily market price. Tracking weight loss during melting and buffing is crucial for accurate profit margins.
  • Multi-Vendor Electroplating & Finishing: Sending semi-finished lock bodies or handles to outside job-workers for plating, powder coating, or anodizing often leads to missing inventory.
  • Dealer Network & Scheme Management: Managing seasonal discount schemes, volume rebates, and credit limits for hundreds of hardware dealers across India.

How a Custom Hardware ERP Solves Production Bottlenecks

1. Real-Time Metal Yield & Scrap Accounting

Track raw metal inputs against finished component outputs to calculate exact scrap percentages and prevent material leakage on the factory floor.

2. Job-Worker & Outward Processing Tracking

Generate digital gate passes when parts leave for zinc plating or polishing, and track returned quantities with automatic defect logging.

3. Master Dealer Portal & WhatsApp Ledger Balance

Hardware stockists and distributors can check their outstanding ledger balances, view dispatch lorry receipts (Bilty numbers), and receive payment receipts on WhatsApp automatically.
