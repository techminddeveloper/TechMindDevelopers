Real Lab Challenges

Daily Pathology Lab Problems We Solve For You

When your diagnostic center tests 50 to 500+ blood samples daily, typing reports in Word, handwriting tube labels, and handling evening counter queues waste hours of clinical staff time.

"Typing test reports in Word takes 15 minutes per patient and causes typing mistakes."

Eliminate manual typing & typos with automated reference intervals

Typing reference ranges, units, and numbers manually in Microsoft Word or Excel for each patient is agonizingly slow. One missed decimal point or typo can cause a dangerous clinical misinterpretation.

How we fix this: Our LIMS auto-loads normal reference ranges according to the patient's age and gender. Technicians enter numbers; abnormal values highlight automatically in bold red, and print-ready PDFs generate in 1 click.

"Handwritten labels on blood tubes cause sample mix-ups and diagnostic panic."

Barcode vacutainer tube tracking from phlebotomy to analyzer

Writing patient names with marker pens on small EDTA, Serum, or Fluoride vials leads to smudged ink, misread handwriting, and the nightmare of running or reporting the wrong patient's blood sample.

How we fix this: Thermal barcode stickers print out instantly at patient registration. A quick scan on the testing computer immediately pulls up the correct patient profile and test panel with zero confusion.

"Patients crowd the reception counter every evening just to collect paper reports."

1-Click automated WhatsApp PDF report delivery direct to phones

Patients and family members call the lab repeatedly asking "Report ban gayi kya?" or travel through traffic and stand in long counter queues just to pick up a single printed sheet of paper.

How we fix this: The instant your MD Pathologist digitally authorizes a report, an encrypted, high-res PDF is delivered directly to the patient's WhatsApp with a personalized message. Counter queues drop by 70%.

"Calculating referring doctor commission ledgers at month-end causes disputes."

Transparent B2B doctor referral accounting & 1-click monthly ledgers

Tracking referral incentives across dozens of doctors, clinics, and nursing homes using rough paper notebooks leads to calculation mistakes, missing cases, and strained medical relationships.

How we fix this: The system automatically tracks doctor referrals per test category. Generate clean, itemized monthly incentive summaries with 1 click, maintaining total financial transparency and trust.
