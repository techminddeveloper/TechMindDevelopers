Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about implementing our custom pathology lab management software.

How does automated WhatsApp test report delivery work?
Once your MD Pathologist or Biochemist digitally approves a test panel, our system generates a clean, encrypted PDF on your official lab header. It immediately triggers the WhatsApp Business API to deliver the report directly to the patient's phone and the referring physician's chat. Patients never have to wait in queues to collect printed sheets.
Does the software support barcode scanning on blood collection tubes?
Yes. During patient registration or home collection, a unique barcode is generated and printed directly onto thermal stickers for each vacutainer tube (EDTA, Serum, Fluoride, etc.). When technicians process samples, scanning the barcode instantly pulls up the correct patient record, eliminating all sample mix-up risks.
Can the software interface directly with automated lab analyzers (Biochemistry / Hematology)?
Yes. We engineer bidirectional and unidirectional machine interfacing using standard ASTM and HL7 protocols (via RS-232 serial or TCP/IP LAN). Results from your automated cell counters and biochemistry analyzers transfer directly into patient test parameters without manual typing.
How does the doctor referral commission tracking work?
Our system includes a confidential B2B Referral Ledger. You can configure custom incentive percentages or fixed amounts per test panel for each referring doctor or collection center. The software tracks total volume and generates automated monthly settlement statements with one-click ledger exports.
Can we import our existing test catalog, formulas, and patient records from Excel?
Yes. As part of our deployment process, our engineering team migrates all your historical patient databases, test names, biological reference ranges, and doctor contact lists from Excel or older legacy software into your new high-performance system.
